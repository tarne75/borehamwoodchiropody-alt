Cookie policy

This site sets no cookies of its own. Here's what that means, and what third parties may do.

About cookies

Cookies are small pieces of text that are stored by your browser. Each cookie has a name and is associated with a particular site. When your browser sends a request to a site (for example, to download a page, image, or video), the computer that responds (known as a server) may tell your browser to set one or more cookies. When your browser makes further requests to the same site it sends the cookies back to the server. This allows the server to remember you as you browse the site.

Cookies set by this site

None. This website is a set of static files. It does not set any first-party cookies, it does not use analytics, and it does not track you between visits. There is therefore no cookie banner and nothing for you to consent to or opt out of.

Third-party connections

Although we set no cookies ourselves, viewing the site causes your browser to connect to a small number of third parties. Those third parties may set their own cookies or otherwise process data, governed by their own policies.

fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com
Google Fonts serves the typeface used across the site. Google states that the Fonts API does not set cookies, but your IP address is processed in order to serve the font files. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
google.com/maps
The map on the contact section is an embedded Google Map, loaded only when you scroll to it. Google may set cookies when this loads. See Google's privacy policy.
formspree.io
Contacted only at the moment you submit the contact form. It processes the details you typed in order to email them to the practice. See Formspree's privacy policy.

Managing cookies

You can block all cookies through your browser settings (see the instructions for Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge). Because this site sets no cookies of its own, blocking them will not stop any part of it working — the embedded map may be affected.
